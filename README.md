Customer Shopping Trends Dashboard (Tableau)
Overview

An interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing customer shopping behavior — covering sales by gender, location, payment method, product category, season, shipping type, and customer review ratings.

Key Questions Answered
How do total sales differ by gender across states?
Which payment methods generate the most sales?
Which products and categories sell the most?
How does sales performance shift across seasons (fall, spring, summer, winter) by category?
How are shipping type and review ratings distributed across seasons?
Tools & Techniques
Tableau Desktop
Multiple worksheets combined into interactive dashboards
Bar charts, pie charts, area charts, word clouds, and bubble charts
Interactive filters (Location, Season, Shipping Type, Review Rating)
Key Insights
Male customers generated higher total sales than female customers across nearly every state (e.g., Missouri: 106,131 vs. 26,925)
Credit Card (1,036,334) and PayPal (1,031,626) were the top payment methods; Bank Transfer was the lowest (889,942)
Coats and Jewelry were the best-selling items (12,670 and 12,331 units)
Clothing was consistently the top-selling category across all seasons, peaking in winter (712,413); Outerwear was the lowest
Review ratings and shipping types were evenly distributed across all four seasons (roughly 24-26% each), suggesting no strong seasonal bias in customer satisfaction

أنهي منتجات وفئات بتتباع أكتر؟
إزاي أداء المبيعات بيتغير عبر المواسم (خريف، ربيع، صيف، شتاء) حسب الفئة؟
